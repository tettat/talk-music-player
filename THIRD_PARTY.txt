Basic Pitch (Spotify): https://github.com/spotify/basic-pitch/tree/v0.4.0
License: Apache-2.0. Full upstream license: analysis/basic_pitch/LICENSE-Spotify.
Five unmodified inference Python files are retained under
analysis/basic_pitch/vendor/basic_pitch/. Model is the 230444-byte ONNX artifact
from the 0.4.0 wheel, kept in .private/models/basic_pitch/ (not public HTTP).
SHA-256 for the wheel, each retained source and model: sources.lock.json.
The local probe.py is separate integration, not a modification of those files.
This uses an ONNX-only subset, not an installed full basic-pitch distribution.

ONNX Runtime, librosa, NumPy, SciPy, soundfile, pretty_midi and supporting packages
are installed only in .private/runtime-analysis. Exact versions are in
analysis/basic_pitch/requirements.lock.txt; their original license notices remain
in the installed distributions. No cloud inference or protected audio decryption.
Model output is an estimate; rights in music and lyrics are not granted by a
model's software license. Private audio and lyrics are not bundled as source.
